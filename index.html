BaldyBrain Dashboard

A personal, single-user plugin for the Obsidian note-taking app.

It adds a dashboard view inside Obsidian with navigation cards, open tasks, recently edited notes, a clock, the weather and a Google Calendar month view with today's agenda.

Google Calendar access

The calendar widgets ask for read-only access to your Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). The plugin can't create, change or delete events. It only reads them so it can show them on the dashboard on your own devices.

See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and where it goes.
